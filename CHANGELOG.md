All notable changes to Tern are documented here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). Tern has no released versions yet; once it does, versions will follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed (PR #57)

Explain in 3D's Next, Play, zoom, Reset and hover no longer close the app (Windows). The cause is now known from the crash log, and it was not the one #55 and #56 inferred.

- **The cause**: `crash.log` (from #56's watchdog) shows `panic at gpui-pre-0.3.9/src/window.rs:5353: called Option::unwrap() on a None value`, then "panic in a function that cannot unwind", exit `0xC0000409`, with `CallWindowProcW` on the stack and "Explain in 3D: step 1 of 16" as the last action. Line 5353 is `Window::current_view()`, which unwraps the top of GPUI's rendered-view stack. `Window::request_animation_frame()` calls it, and the stack holds a view only while GPUI renders, prepaints or paints one. Tern asked for the next frame with `request_animation_frame()` inside click, mouse, wheel and pinch listeners, where the stack is empty, so it panicked; on Windows a listener runs inside the window procedure, an `extern` function that cannot unwind, so the process aborted. Linux and macOS would have panicked too, on the same clicks.
- **Corrected**: #56's "stack overflow on the 1 MB Windows main thread" and #55's "label layout and batching" were inferences, made without a Windows crash report. Neither was the cause of the closing. The 8 MB main-thread stack, the path and label budgets and the label layout work stay: they are sound on their own, just not the fix for this crash.
- **Now**: Tern asks for frames with `view::frame::next_frame(window, cx)`, which notifies its own view (`cx.entity_id()`) once the next frame is drawn, and `next_frame_for(window, id)` in the paint callbacks and popover content that have no `Context`. Both are correct in a listener, a timer, an action, a task or a render. Every call was changed, also the ones that ran in a render and were safe, so the habit cannot come back: Explain in 3D's step click, pointer move (hover), press, release, wheel zoom, pinch, the stage-size and error repaints in its canvas, the level and step dots, Reset, Play and Next; the timeline rail's click glide (it also crashed); the workspace's render-time requests (flashcard turn, theme wipe and sliding panels, highlight and knobs), the toast, Scout and Explain ticks, the context ring's sweep, the run map's figure, and the inner-list wheel listener (`wheel_first`, which read `current_view()` in a paint callback and now takes the view's id).
- **Guard**: `dependency_rules` fails if `request_animation_frame` or `current_view(` appears anywhere in a crate's source (outside the tests in `view/frame.rs`), and separately if either appears inside a `cx.listener(...)` or `.on_*(...)` body; the scan's own test shows it catches the bad pattern. GPUI test-app tests (`TestAppContext`, GPUI's `test-support` as a dev-dependency) click, wheel and play on a stand-in stage and deliver frames with `simulate_next_frame`, and show that `request_animation_frame` outside a render panics.
- Audited, no change needed: GPUI's own elements read `current_view` while they lay out, prepaint and paint (`div`, `img`, `list`, `text`, `with_animation`, `use_asset`, `use_keyed_state`, `defer_draw`), and Tern builds and paints elements only from renders and canvas callbacks, never from a listener. The crash watchdog stays.

### Fixed and added (PR #56)

Explain in 3D no longer closes the app on Next or Play (Windows), crashes that no panic hook sees are now logged, inner lists scroll with the wheel, Tern opens the way it was left, and the macOS CI flake is gone.

- **Next or Play in Explain in 3D closed the app within a second (Windows), in Research and Study, with no error card.** The scene loaded and the first step drew; the step change killed the process. The `catch_unwind` guards from #55 never fired, so it was not a panic. It could not be reproduced here (no Windows GPU build in this sandbox); the cause is inferred:
  - Not `panic = "abort"`: no profile in the workspace sets it, so the guards do catch panics.
  - Not recursion or big stack buffers in Tern's step, label or glide code: the step code path (20 parts, 15 steps, every step there and back, Play, three stage sizes) runs on a 64 KB stack in a release build, and on 1 MB in a debug build.
  - **Inferred root cause (wrong, corrected in #57: it was a panic in GPUI's `current_view`): a stack overflow on the Windows main thread.** Windows gives an executable's main thread 1 MB (Linux and macOS give 8 MB, which is why only Windows crashed). GPUI lays out and paints the element tree recursively on that thread, with large frames per level; the Explain stage with its labels, caption and step bar sits deep inside the workspace, and the step change adds to it. A stack overflow ends the process at once (`0xC00000FD`), with nothing for a panic hook to catch or write. Zed, built on GPUI, links its Windows executable with an 8 MB stack for the same reason.
  - **Now**: `tern-desktop.exe` is linked with an 8 MB main-thread stack (`build.rs`: `/STACK:8388608` on MSVC, `--stack` on GNU; `dependency_rules` keeps it).
  - **Paths are checked before they reach the renderer**: every point finite and near the stage, at most 256 points a path, no empty paths, at most 12,000 paths a frame; label boxes with a non-finite or absurd size are dropped.
  - **Crashes no panic hook sees are logged (Windows release builds)**: Tern starts as a small watchdog that runs the app as a child process (plain `std::process`, no unsafe code). When the app ends with anything but success, the watchdog writes the exit code and what it means (stack overflow, fail-fast abort, access violation, device removed, heap corruption), the last thing the app was doing (`last-action.txt`, written on each Explain step change: "Explain in 3D: step 2 of 15 (20 parts, 31 links)") and the end of the app's error output (`stderr.log`, where Rust prints "has overflowed its stack") to `crash.log`. `TERN_NO_WATCHDOG=1` turns it off. The logs are in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Tern\data\logs\` (`crash.log`, `tern.log`, `stderr.log`, `last-action.txt`).
  - Tests: the user's kind of scene through every step change, Play and back on a 1 MB-stack thread (also its own step on the Windows CI job), the exit-code report, the stack rule.
- **macOS CI flake**: `degenerate_scenes_survive_steps_play_and_zoom` failed on a shared runner with "frame 5868 took 74.78ms". The Explain tests no longer time frames with the wall clock; they count work instead (a frame lays labels out at most once, a step costs one layout, never one per frame).
- **Inner lists did not scroll with the mouse wheel or touchpad** (Settings › Providers' model list, the model catalogue, a role's choices, the first-run model list); only dragging the thumb moved them, and the page behind scrolled instead. Root cause: the kit's scroll areas (`overflow_y_scrollbar`) take the wheel in the capture phase, before anything inside them, and stop it there; a plain GPUI list inside one only scrolls in the bubble phase, which never came. Each inner list now takes the wheel first (`view::wheel_first`): it scrolls by the turn (lines or precise touchpad pixels), keeps it from the page, and at its ends hands it on to the page (`scrollbar::wheel_take`, unit tested). Audited: the model picker popover, the slash and mention menus, the Library, the Explain caption and the Study pages use the kit's own areas (the innermost under the pointer already wins); the Study screen and the chat transcript are not nested in another area.
- **Opens the way it was left**: the harness (Research or Study), each harness's chat tabs with the one in front (its chat opens first; a chat deleted or archived since falls back to the last chat with messages, then a new chat), the active Study tab (Chat, Flashcards, Quiz, Viva, Explain), the right panel's view and whether it was open, and whether the sidebar was open. Saved 0.8 s after a change and again, on the spot, when the window closes, in `preferences.json` under the data folder (`Preferences::session`, `crate::restore`, unit tested). Before, a restart always opened Research, often on a New chat tab.

### Fixed (PR #55)

Explain in 3D no longer freezes and closes the app on Play, Next or zoom (Windows), the loading line shows it is working, and the Windows taskbar shows the Tern mark.

- **Play, Next and zooming froze Tern, then it closed (Windows).** Any camera or step change hung the window: after the click it never repainted (the Play button never turned into Pause) and the process ended about 8 s later. It could not be reproduced here (no Windows GPU build in this sandbox), so this is what the code did and what changed:
  - Each camera position was its own cached frame, so every frame of a glide, a zoom or an orbit projected the scene again and laid every label out again, then built every path again: the seam stroke of every face, each dashed link cut into its dashes, and so on, at the display rate.
  - The labels drawn over the canvas sat between the paths' draw orders, so the renderer split the scene's paths into many batches. On Windows each batch clears and resolves a window-sized multisampled texture.
  - Only the canvas painter was guarded against panics. A panic in a click, wheel or key handler, in the step timer, or in the frame's own work unwinds into the window procedure, and on Windows the process aborts. Windows Error Reporting then holds the frozen window for a few seconds before it closes, which is what the recording shows.
  - **Now**: labels are laid out once for where the camera is going, right away for a new step, pointer node or stage size, and 150 ms after the camera's target last moved for a drag, a wheel turn or a pinch (`RELAYOUT_AFTER`). In between, each label rides with its node. A step costs one layout, not one per glide frame. While the camera moves the painter leaves out dashes and seam strokes, and an invisible quad above the paths keeps them in one batch. `labels::place` takes at most 64 labels (`MAX_LABELS`), so its work is bounded. Label text size moves in half-pixel steps. Every handler (step, Play, keys, wheel, pinch, pointer, dots, levels, Reset), the autoplay timer and the frame's work run under `catch_unwind`; a panic shows the error card with **Try again**. Play is one timer for all tabs, started at most once.
  - **For the next report**: every panic is written with its place and a backtrace to `<data>/logs/crash.log` (`crashlog`), and an Explain frame slower than 50 ms is noted in `<data>/logs/tern.log`. Both stay on this computer.
- **Less tangled maps**: a link touching the step's nodes is drawn in full (dashed, with a head), a link joining two consecutive steps is drawn lightly, and the rest are nearly invisible.
- **Loading**: the hourglass turns over (repainted about 12 times a second by a timer, not every frame; still with reduced motion, when only the seconds change). The line says where the build is ("Gathering your sources…", "Asking the model… (part 2 of 3)", "Reading the reply…", "Laying out the scene…"; `tern_agent::explain::Stage`, carried on the build's cancel token) and how many seconds it has taken.
- **Windows icon**: `tern-desktop.exe`, `tern.exe` and `tern-browser.exe` embed the Tern mark (`assets/brand/tern.ico`: 16, 24, 32, 48, 64 and 256 px, white on black, the small sizes sharpened) and version information through a `build.rs` with `winresource`, a Windows-only build dependency. GPUI's window class uses icon resource 1, so the taskbar and title bar show it. The browser helper sets it on its tao window too.
- Tests: every step and the whole Play run on a 20-node, 16-step, densely linked scene (one layout per step, each frame within a time budget), zoom, pinch and key sequences (no layout until the target stays put, then one), an orbit drag, the links' weights, degenerate scenes (everything on one spot, NaN and huge numbers, 200 parts, one part) through steps, Play, zoom and odd stage sizes, `labels::place` with 1,000 labels, the build stages, and the icon resources in `dependency_rules`.

### Fixed (PR #54)

Explain in 3D no longer freezes or crashes the app, popovers draw over the page, and scenes are readable.

- **Explain in 3D froze the app while the scene loaded, then it crashed (Windows).** What the code did: the loading state animated a spinner, so the whole window was redrawn every frame for as long as the model took (a small local model mapping a long answer made up to four sequential 4,000-token calls with no time limit); once the scene arrived, every frame projected the scene again, laid out every label again (16 to 20 nodes plus key terms, re-shaped each frame) and built a tessellated path for every face, edge and dashed link, including ones off the stage; and the painter ran inside the window procedure with no guard, so a panic there (a degenerate figure, or a path the tessellator rejects) aborted the process instead of failing one frame. Now: the scene is built on the background runtime with a cancel token (**Cancel** while building, a closed tab or a new build stops the old one, a cancelled build never lands) and a 150 s limit per model call (past it Tern maps the answer itself); a panic while building comes back as an error card with **Try again**; every scene is sanitised before it is drawn (`tern_study::explain3d::sanitize`: finite numbers, parts on the floor, unique ids, at most 40 parts and 40 steps, steps and links naming real parts); the camera ignores NaN input, clamps pan, and goes home if a pose ever stops being finite; the drawing and the placed labels are cached per camera position, size and step (`ExplainState::frame`), so a still scene costs nothing and labels are laid out once per change; the painter skips non-finite or far-off points and shapes outside the stage, never dashes a link longer than 4,000 px, and catches a panic (the tab then shows the error card). The loading line is still text with **Cancel**, not an animation. Tests for empty, single-point, NaN and oversized scenes in `tern-study` and the desktop's `explain` module.
- **The model's map**: small models' near-JSON is read (`tern_study::text::repair_json`: code fences, text before and after, single and curly quotes, bare keys, trailing commas, Python literals, a reply cut off mid-list); the node list may be called `nodes`, `steps`, `items` or `parts`, or be a bare list, and levels may be names ("Foundations", "core idea").
- **Readable scenes**: the figure (not the whole floor) is framed to fit the stage on load and on **Reset view**; an answer's map opens turned so each level is a row across the screen, basics at the front, with lower nodes; labels are placed without overlaps (`tern_study::labels`): the step's nodes (and the one under the pointer or clicked) get their full label, linked nodes and the step's level theirs when there is room, the rest their step number, and a label that had to move keeps a leader line to its node; label size follows the zoom between 11 and 15 px.
- **Header**: the title takes the room left and wraps to two lines, then ends in "…" (the full title on hover); Save, Save to vault and SVG keep to the right and move to their own row on narrow tabs.
- **Popovers drawn under the page**: the tab strip's **+** popover (New chat, Open existing chat, search) was absolutely positioned in the tab strip, so the 3D view's buttons and caption painted over it. It is now in the overlay layer (`deferred`, above everything), opaque with a border and shadow, anchored under **+**, closed by a press outside or Esc, and keyboard navigable (Up, Down, Enter). The tab overflow menu (**N ▾**), the Library's **New ▾** and the timeline's hover card moved to the overlay layer too. Audited: the model picker, effort slider, harness switcher and the run panel's **More** menu were already in it; the slash and mention menus, the message action menu and **Branch ▾** sit in the page's flow, so nothing draws over them.
- **Study**: the Explain view lays out above the composer (it shrinks; nothing runs under the composer), and the camera help is a **?** with a tooltip instead of a long line. Explain in 3D asked from a quiz question ("Here's your first question… A) … B) …") maps the last answer that explains something; when there is none, the Explain tab says so and asks what to explain.

### Added and changed (PR #53)

The browser in its own process (fixes the Windows freeze), chat tabs, the v2 Graph run map, and a deeper Explain in 3D.

- **Fixed: clicking Browser froze Tern on Windows, then it crashed after 30 to 40 s.** Root cause: wry built WebView2 synchronously from inside GPUI's frame (the page canvas's prepaint), waiting in a nested message pump on GPUI's UI thread. Re-entrant paints were dropped (the window looked frozen), clicks and Close reached callbacks already taken for the frame, and a GPUI task finishing inside the pump (the crawler's read of the page) borrowed app state the frame still held; the panic inside a window procedure aborted the process. The page engine now runs in a helper process, **`tern-browser`** (wry + tao, its own event loop; WebView2, WKWebView, WebKitGTK), driven over a line-based JSON protocol (`tern_core::browser_ipc`) that the desktop only ever writes to and reads from on background threads. A helper that does not report ready in 5 s, or stops, leaves the pane with the reader view and **Restart**. On Windows the page window is owned by Tern's; Wayland gets an attached window. The helper ships next to `tern-desktop` in every artifact ([ADR 0011](docs/decisions/0011-browser-helper-process.md); `dependency_rules` keeps engine crates out of the front ends).
- **`tern browse <address or words>`**: the reader view of a page and Tern's cited explanation, or search results; `--open` opens the helper window.
- **Chat tabs** with a **+** popover (new chat, recent chats), per harness, restored on launch (`Preferences::open_tabs`).
- **Graph** (right panel): FIG 1 · RUN MAP, a hairline isometric figure of the run (a hub slab, one box per phase, cube clusters for sources and reads, dotted links to phases not reached, the live link in the accent with a travelling dot), lit phase by phase from the run's events; a readout (phase 4/6 · verify, sources · kept · spend; hover a phase for its numbers, click it to open the view with its steps), and spend over time against the budget with a crosshair tooltip. Lines draw in; reduced motion shows them at once. Only searches that ran count as sources.
- **Explain in 3D, deeper**: a whole chat answer is mapped from the basics to the advanced parts (4 to 20 nodes in five levels, sized to the answer, key-term sub-nodes, prerequisite links, each node with its explanation, passage and citations) through `tern_study::explain_answer`; no 5,000-character cut (long answers go to the model in chunks and the maps merge). A real camera (`tern_study::camera`): orbit with a full turn and a clamped tilt, wheel or pinch zoom about the pointer, right- or Shift-drag pan, double-click to focus a node, Reset view, arrows and + / −, damped inertia, reduced motion respected. Prev / Next / Play walk the levels with the camera gliding onto each step, and a side caption reads the step or the clicked node. **Explain in 3D** moved out of More ▾ into the visible row under every answer, in Research and Study.
- Deferred: renaming the right-panel views to v2's Plan · Sources N · Report · Graph · Context (the views do not map one to one; Context and Plan are new views, not renames).

### Added and changed (PR #52)

Study as one window, Research and Study kept apart, a real browser, and compaction for small windows.

- **Built-in browser** ([ADR 0010](docs/decisions/0010-browser-engine.md)): tabs, an address bar that takes an address or words, back, forward, reload, and a **Page | Explanation | Side by side** toggle (side by side by default). The Page side is the real site in the system web view through `wry` (WebView2 on Windows, WKWebView on macOS), a native child of Tern's window kept on the pane's bounds and hidden whenever Settings, a modal, the switcher, or Explanation-only shows; links clicked inside it move the address bar, tab title and explanation along. Linux (no engine in this build) and a failed engine start show Tern's reader view instead. The Explanation side is the page as Tern Crawl reads it, or search results to open for words typed in the bar, with **Explain with citations** (or **Explain briefly** for words) asking Tern; the answer shows above the page text. Citations and source rows open in the pane.
- **Study in one window**: a sliding tab bar (Chat | Flashcards | Quiz | Viva | Explain in 3D) at the top, the mode in the middle and the Study composer at the bottom; no separate Study chat and Study tools screens. The composer can drive a mode ("quiz me on paging", "flashcards for chapter 3", "examine me", "explain TLBs in 3D"). Modes that need materials show Add material, Upload PDF, Use the last report and Paste link. Home tiles wrap and every tile opens its mode; a status line shows what is due and recall.
- **Research and Study kept apart**: the sidebar lists only the active harness's chats, a chat of the other harness switches harness first, and each harness reopens its last chat, draft, scroll and panels (`Preferences::last_chats`), also after a restart. The Research | Study pill moved from the title bar to a switcher at the top of the sidebar. A deleted chat is forgotten by both harnesses.
- **Compaction on small windows**: the verbatim tail is bounded by 35 % of the model's window as well as the turn count, so a short chat on a 4k model still folds; when nothing can be freed the toast says why.
- **Reply** shows the quoted text as a chip over the bubble; **Scout** is 64 px with its state named beside it; model lists in Settings keep a visible scrollbar and hold the wheel.

### Fixed and added (PR #49)

### Added (PR #38)

- **Decision model** (`tern-decision`, GPUI-free, tested offline): the phase 8 slot, `score(context, question, choices) -> probabilities`. A `DecisionRequest` (kind: source ranking, next tool, claim check, now or later, general; context; question; choices with optional priors) becomes a `Decision`: probabilities summing to 1, the chosen choice, confidence, a rationale, the model and provider, latency, input tokens and dollars, any fallback reason, any hard-check overrides, and whether it came from the cache.

Cohere and Jina rerank were not integrated: their per-request prices could not be verified on their own pages.

Offline: fixed order 52%, local scorer 86% (in-sample; claims tie at 9/16).

- **Plan approval**: the plan is put to the user before anything is searched or read: **Approve** (run as drafted), **Edit** (reword or drop sub-questions, then **Run**), **Skip plan** (one worker on the whole question), or **Cancel**. Events `plan.drafted` and `plan.answered` (with `edited`).
- **Parallel workers** (`tern_agent::deep`): each sub-question runs as a worker, up to 3 at once (`deep_workers` in preferences, 1 to 6). A worker searches its planned queries, reads up to 3 new pages per round through the shared Tern Crawl reader (robots.txt, per-host pacing, page cache), and writes short notes citing its pages; it may ask for one more search. Each keeps to its own slice of tokens, pages, and time (Over budget and Timed out keep the notes so far), every search and read still goes through the policy gate, and each can be cancelled alone (`DeepControl::cancel_worker`) or with the whole run. Only its notes and their pages reach the synthesis. Events `worker.started` and `worker.finished`; its searches, reads, and model calls are logged as usual with a `worker` field.
- **Synthesis**: the workers' notes are merged into one numbering (the same page read by two workers gets one number), one answer is written from them, its citations are renumbered in order of first use with made-up numbers removed, and a `Sources` list is appended. The claim checks and run report then run as after any turn; the report gains the plan and a **Workers** section (sub-question, status, searches, pages of its slice, tokens of its slice, time, pages read).
- `tern research --deep "question" [--workers N] [--yes] [--json]`: shows the plan and asks to run, edit, skip, or cancel it, prints worker progress, then the answer, the verdict tally, and the report's path.
- Desktop: a **Deep research** toggle in the composer (Research harness), a plan card in the transcript (sub-questions with their searches, source types, and shares, the preflight range, Approve / Edit / Skip plan / Cancel; edit mode has a field and a drop button per sub-question and **Run**), and a **Workers** view in the run panel: one row per worker with its status, sub-question, pages read with each site's logo, pages and tokens against its slice, and a cancel button. The working line says "Researching · 2 running · 1 of 3 done".
- `Run` carries a `DeepRun` (plan, plan state, workers) rebuilt from the events, so a reopened chat shows the same workers; `StepKind::Plan` and `StepKind::Worker`.

### Added (PR #33)

- **Claim verdicts** (`tern-research`, GPUI-free and tested offline): after each answer, Tern finds its claims (every sentence with a `[n]` citation; the closing link list is read for which page each number means, never as claims) and checks each against the text Tern Crawl read for the pages it cites. Verdicts: **Supported**, **Partly supported**, **Not supported**, **No source**, each with the quoted evidence passage and an anchor to it (a `#:~:text=` fragment, or `#page=N` for a PDF page). Two paths: the chat's own model judges the best three passages per claim (one call, up to 16 claims, temperature 0), and a deterministic word-overlap check (content words, every number present, negation agreeing) gives the verdict offline and fills in for anything the model does not answer. Seeded fixtures cover supported, misquoted number, contradicted, unrelated, and unreachable sources.
- **Run reports**: each answered turn writes `runs/<chat>/turn-<n>.md` and `.json` in the data folder: the question, plan and steps, every source read with its reader (Tern Crawl), robots.txt result, and cache hit or miss, the claims with verdicts and evidence, model calls, tool calls, tokens and time against the turn's budget, and errors. Built from the event log alone (`tern_agent::report`), so any past turn can be rebuilt.
- `tern report [chat] [--turn N] [--json] [--out FILE] [--list]` prints or exports a report (`last` by default, or a chat id or its start). A turn that was never checked is checked then, by word overlap.
- Desktop: a verdict badge after each checked claim in the answer (click to open that claim in the report), a **Report** view in the run panel (tally, each claim with its verdict glyph, quoted evidence, and its source row with the site's logo; **Open report** and **JSON** open the saved files), and the working line says "Checking 3 claims" while it runs. The TUI logs the check too.
- Events `claims.checking` and `claims.checked` (verdicts, tally, report paths, judge tokens); `read_url` events carry a `crawl` block (reader, robots, cache). `Fetcher::has_cached`.

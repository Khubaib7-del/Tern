# Brand logos

Most marks in this folder come from [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons)
(`@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.98.0, MIT licence, © LobeHub). Tern changed
only their size attributes and removed the inline `style` and `<title>`.
Where Lobe Icons has only a one-colour drawing of a brand that has colours,
Tern filled the Lobe Icons shapes with the colours of the brand's own
official icon (noted below). Two marks are the brands' own files.
